# Privacy Policy — Kids Memory Match

*Last updated: September 22, 2026*

Kids Memory Match ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed as a safe, private, educational game for children and families.

### 1. Zero Data Collection
Kids Memory Match does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, identifiers, device information, or usage analytics.

### 2. No Internet Access
The application does not request the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission and operates 100% offline. The app is incapable of transmitting any data over the internet.

### 3. No Advertisements or Third-Party SDKs
There are no third-party advertisements, tracking pixels, analytical libraries, or external services integrated into Kids Memory Match.

### 4. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) Compliance
Our application fully complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Designed for Families policy. Because we collect zero data from all users including children under 13, no personal data is ever collected or processed.

### 5. Local Data Storage
Game progress (such as unlocked levels and high scores) and user preferences (such as sound and music toggles) are stored strictly locally on the user's device using Android's DataStore. This information is never uploaded or shared with any server.

### 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us at:
